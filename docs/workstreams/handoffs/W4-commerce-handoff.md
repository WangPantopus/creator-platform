## Important fixes that must survive continuation

- Ordinary fans cannot use `SELECT FOR SHARE` on catalog rows governed by creator-only UPDATE RLS: PostgreSQL also applies UPDATE policies. Mode reads use shared transaction advisory key `commerce.mode:<id>` and plain SELECT; `saveMode` uses its exclusive counterpart. Reauthorization checks the current effective proposed mode while holding that key. Preserve mode → capacity/packet/share ordering. Tier readers similarly use shared `commerce.tier:<id>`; all future tier writers must use the exclusive counterpart.
- Billing account locks precede membership locks; pass locks precede slot locks. Provider I/O occurs outside SQL locks. Capture aggregate versions before provider reads and reject/refetch stale results. A stale active membership/pass/store response must not restore access after cancellation/refund; a stale no-dispute statement must not clear a newer hold.
- Payment/billing/payout/inbox processing has durable keys, leases and attempt fencing. Old completion/error responses cannot overwrite a newer attempt. Unknown external money stays processing until verified current truth; do not retry with a fresh key after an ambiguous operation.
- Reauthorization uses the same packet and append-only authorization attempt lineage, exact new disclosure/current-mode consent and bank deadline. More-info pauses the decision SLA, never the bank clock; acceptance is bounded by actual provider capture expiry minus six hours.
- Exact signed human or approved-draft evidence is necessary for a personal promise. AI/team/wrong-media/edited draft/incomplete call cannot fulfill it. Genuine capture creates one commitment; synthetic client outcomes cannot create delivery.
- Same paid period restore/replay preserves used/reserved allowance and first-used evidence. Only a new genuinely paid period resets usage. A refunded paid period cannot reactivate. Terminal subscriptions require a genuinely new fenced subscription under the same customer with no live old items; old references/history remain immutable. Pass purchase replacement is owned, terminal and nonoverlapping.
- Removing creator per-mode sharing permission atomically revokes existing grants and emits versioned invalidations. Fan choice alone cannot override current permission. Revoked cards do not restore themselves when a mode becomes shareable again.
- Paid AI/trials remain gated until actual cost reservations are integrated; offered/submitted nonwritten promises require a configured actual producer. Confirmed request submission records membership first-use; W3 AI/Note first-use is still missing. Do not turn gates off to manufacture a sandbox success.
- Read-credit issuance and refund clawback share wallet/cause locks. The configured gross monthly noncash cap cannot refill after clawback; credits never become cash. Pool allocations serialize per creator/cycle and preserve exact integer totals.
- W8 refund commands throw while outcome is pending/unknown; a case may complete only after its exact confirmed refund ledger cause exists. Financial exports include records without packet IDs and linked grant counters, throw above bounds, and require real purpose authority for restricted creators. Do not acknowledge incomplete export/deletion.
