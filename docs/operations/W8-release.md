## Store submission preparation

Policies checked against official sources on 2026-09-29; recheck at submission. Apple requires in-app deletion initiation and disclosure of retained/delayed data. Apple billing continues separately, so provide subscription management while preserving immediate deletion. Google requires discoverable in-app and external web deletion for covered accounts; directing account creation through Pantopus does not establish an exemption. Google requires reporting generated offensive content from within the app. [Apple deletion](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app), [Google deletion](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en), [Google AI policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13985936?hl=en).

Prepare accurate app/developer identity, functional privacy/deletion/support URLs, moderation/contact procedures, sandbox review accounts with only synthetic data, and reviewer steps through sign-in → labeled AI → report → support/privacy → billing management. Do not submit accounts, final URLs or declarations until configured. Apple UGC moderation and explicit permission for third-party AI data sharing require actual implementation; consent must name configured processors. [Apple review guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).

Audit the actual signed archive and SDK dependencies for collected/shared data, tracking and required-reason APIs. Neither a placeholder privacy page nor a generated manifest proves accurate store declarations. Keep third-party SDK data paths in Apple privacy labels and Google Data safety, including off-device ephemeral processing. [Apple privacy labels](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-privacy), [Apple manifests](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/privacy-manifest-files), [Google Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en).

## Rollout and rollback

Run G0–G5 against the same immutable staging revision; collect each owner's actual-app proof. Assign incident commander, safety/commerce/identity/database/AI responders with staffed hours and contact channels. Establish budgets with business input; proposed thresholds in `infra/alerts.json` are not staffed alerts.

Release by capability flag and bounded cohort only after approval. Compare error/latency/queue/billing/privacy/author-comprehension metrics with the baseline. Stop expansion for any isolation failure, unresolved money, five-second revocation miss, false authorship, privacy reopening or unavailable mandatory support. Roll back the application to a compatible revision; leave additive schema, tombstones and idempotency records intact. Destructive-schema recovery uses a new isolated restore environment and replay gate.

Incident draft: “We are investigating [capability] from [UTC time]. [Specific user impact]. [Verified workaround]. Next update: [time].” Closure states actual restoration time and remaining retries. Public notices never include fan text, private reasons or secrets. Human responders approve external communications.
