## Primary-source research used for the plan

Retrieved 2026-09-29. Sources support technical constraints and opportunities, not a promise that these additions will improve retention. Market claims and proposed pilot percentages in the earlier Second Review are retained as hypotheses until measured.

- **Discovery:** Google documents crawlable link structure and eligible profile-page structured data. Plan implication: server-render meaningful public creator content, link it coherently and validate truthful metadata; private threads and paid source text stay inaccessible. There is no guaranteed search placement. [Site structure](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/help-google-understand-your-ecommerce-site-structure), [ProfilePage documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/profile-page).
- **Payment recovery:** Stripe documents duplicate and out-of-order events, signed raw-body verification and asynchronous handling. Use durable inbox/reconciliation, not event arrival order as financial truth. Hold windows depend on the actual payment method/network and capture deadline; read the payment's capture-before value. [Webhooks](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks), [Separate authorization and capture](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method).
- **Accessible completion:** WCAG 2.2 includes focus-not-obscured, target-size, redundant-entry and accessible-authentication criteria. These matter for signing sheets, chat composers, sticky controls and onboarding recovery. The proposed standard adds actual assistive-technology walkthroughs to visual comparison. [W3C WCAG 2.2 changes](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/new-in-22/).
- **AI security:** OWASP's LLM risks include prompt injection, sensitive disclosure and unbounded consumption. Plan implication: enforce source/actor scope outside the model, treat retrieved text as untrusted, constrain provider capabilities and costs, and inspect adversarial runtime outcomes. No classifier is assumed to eliminate these risks. [OWASP LLM risks](https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/), [Prompt injection prevention](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/LLM_Prompt_Injection_Prevention_Cheat_Sheet.html).
- **Native trust/release:** Apple/Google primary documentation on credentials, association files, deletion, privacy, payments, notifications, calling, SDKs and page-size compatibility is linked claim-by-claim in the [platform research](research/platform-inventory.md#current-official-platform-constraints). Revalidate policy and storefront applicability before submission. These are engineering inputs, not counsel approval.

## Measurement without manipulating users

Track fan arrival→useful answer, cited-source opens, return, voluntary membership/renewal, successful human delivery, refund/support causes and authorship comprehension. Track creator activation, Notes cadence, personal effort, fulfilled deadlines and desire to continue. Separate expert and companion cohorts; a single blended conversion number hides product fit.

Use the source pilot's proposed thresholds as a starting discussion, not industry benchmarks or a reason to abandon the full build. Define denominators, observation window and minimum meaningful sample before evaluating. Retention gains do not count as success if accompanied by confusing AI identity, increased distress/dependency, hidden cancellation, privacy loss or excessive creator labor. Collect minimal event data and consented feedback; sensitive conversation content is not marketing telemetry.
